Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Public Retirement Planner, which is owned and operated by X Ventures LLC ("we", "this site"), collects and how it is used. The short version: the numbers you type into our tools never leave your device, and we use Google AdSense to show ads, which uses cookies.

What you type into the tools

Our calculators run entirely in your web browser. Your answers, such as your age, pay and account balances, are not sent to us or to anyone else. To save you retyping, your browser may remember your answers and your text and color settings on your own device (using "local storage"). You can clear them at any time with the "Start over" button or by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Other information

Our web host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. We do not sell personal information.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you.

Children

This site is meant for adults planning for retirement and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads using the links above. Residents of some states, such as California, have additional rights under state law. To ask a question about your privacy, contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
