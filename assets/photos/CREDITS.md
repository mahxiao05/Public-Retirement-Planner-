# Photo credits and licenses

Every photo on the site is listed here with where it came from and the license
it was used under. Only use photos that allow free commercial use:

- Pexels License (https://www.pexels.com/license/)
- Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license), never Unsplash+ photos
- Public domain, such as work by US federal government employees

Rules for this site:

- No photo may suggest that the person in it uses, recommends or works for the
  site. Never put a photo next to a quote, review or name.
- No credit is shown on the site (neither license requires one). Keep the
  photo page link below so the license can be checked later.
- Process new photos with `python3 tools/photos.py SOURCE NAME` and give the
  hero photo a plain alt text.
