Contact us

Public Pension Planner is owned and operated by X Ventures LLC. We'd like to hear from you, especially if you find a number that looks wrong or a rule that has changed.

Email

info@publicpensionplanner.com

We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Please don't send personal details

Don't include your Social Security number, member ID, account numbers or other private information. We can't look up your records.

Questions about your own benefits

For your official benefit amounts, contact your retirement system directly:

  • Oregon PERS: 888-320-7377, or oregon.gov/pers
  • Social Security: 800-772-1213, or ssa.gov/myaccount
  • Federal retirement (OPM): 888-767-6738, or opm.gov/retirement-center
  • Thrift Savings Plan: 877-968-3778, or tsp.gov
