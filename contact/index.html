Questions about your own benefits

For your official benefit amounts, contact your retirement system directly:

  • Oregon PERS: 888-320-7377, or oregon.gov/pers
  • Social Security: 800-772-1213, or ssa.gov/myaccount
  • Federal retirement (OPM): 888-767-6738, or opm.gov/retirement-center
  • Thrift Savings Plan: 877-968-3778, or tsp.gov
