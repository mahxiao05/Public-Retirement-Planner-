Common questions

Which calculator should I start with?

If you want a quick answer, start with Can I retire? It takes about three minutes. If you want the full picture with your spouse and several retirement dates side by side, use the Retirement Date Planner.

Are the calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator is free, and you don't need an account. The site is paid for by ads, which never change the math.

Where do the numbers come from?

From the official rules: Oregon PERS, Washington DRS, OPM, the TSP, Social Security and the IRS. Each calculator shows every step of the math and links to the rule it uses.

Can I trust the result for my retirement date?

Use it to compare choices and to know what to ask. Before you pick a date, get an official estimate from your retirement system and check your Social Security statement at ssa.gov/myaccount.
