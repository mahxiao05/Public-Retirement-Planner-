FERS retirement calculator

Updated October 31, 2026

Short answer

The FERS basic annuity is your high-3 average salary × your years of service × 1%. It rises to 1.1% if you retire at 62 or older with at least 20 years. Enter your numbers to see your monthly pension and what each extra year of work adds.

Your numbers

Your high-3 and service are on your agency's retirement estimate or your annual benefits statement.

    What this calculator leaves out

    • The FERS supplement. If you retire before 62 on an unreduced annuity, FERS adds a supplement until 62. The full planner estimates it. See the supplement and the earnings test.
    • A survivor annuity. A full one costs 10% of your annuity and a partial one 5%. See FERS survivor benefit.
    • Raises after you retire. Most regular FERS retirees get no cost-of-living raise until 62. See the FERS COLA.
    • Military buyback and part-time service. Use our military buyback calculator, and ask your HR office about part-time months.
    See a worked example

    Pat was born in 1970, so Pat's Minimum Retirement Age is 57. Pat has 25 years of service and a high-3 of $85,000.

    • At 57: Pat can retire under MRA+10, but the annuity is cut 5% for each year under 62, so 25% off.
    • At 60: with 20 or more years, the cut is gone. $85,000 × 28 years × 1% = $23,800 a year, about $1,983 a month.
    • At 62: the multiplier rises to 1.1%. $85,000 × 30 years × 1.1% = $28,050 a year, about $2,338 a month.

    Common questions

    How is the FERS pension calculated?

    High-3 average salary × years of service × 1%, or 1.1% if you retire at 62 or older with at least 20 years. Law enforcement officers, firefighters and air traffic controllers retiring under special provisions get 1.7% for their first 20 years and 1% after that.

    What is my Minimum Retirement Age?

    55 if you were born before 1948, rising by two months a year to 56 for 1953 to 1964, then by two months a year to 57 for 1970 or later.

    Can I retire at my MRA with 10 years?

    Yes, under MRA+10, but your annuity is cut 5/12 of 1% for each month you are under 62 (5% a year) unless you postpone the start. With 20 years, postponing to 60 removes the cut.

    Does unused sick leave count?

    Yes, in the formula. OPM converts your hours into extra months of service using a 2,087-hour work year. It does not help you reach the years you need to be eligible.

    Who gives an official FERS estimate?

    Your agency's HR or benefits office. Ask for an annuity estimate a year or two before you plan to leave.
