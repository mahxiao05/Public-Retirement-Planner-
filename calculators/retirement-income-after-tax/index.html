What will my take-home pay be in retirement?

Tax figures for 2026, checked October 18, 2026, against irs.gov

Short answer

Your pension and savings withdrawals are taxed as income. Up to 85% of Social Security can be taxed too, depending on your other income. For 2026, people 65 and older get a larger standard deduction plus a new $6,000 senior deduction (2025 through 2028). Enter your amounts to see what you'd keep each month.

Your yearly amounts

PERS, DRS, FERS or other, before tax.
From the TSP, IAP, 457, 403(b) or traditional IRAs. Leave out Roth money.
Part-time pay, interest, dividends.
For both of you, if married.
Washington has no income tax, so use 0. Oregon taxes pensions at rates from 4.75% to 9.9%; your average rate is lower than your top rate. See state taxes.
Medicare Part B, a supplement or retiree plan.

How we worked this out

    What this estimate leaves out

    • After-tax contributions. If you paid into your pension with money that was already taxed, part of each payment comes back tax free. Your retirement system's 1099-R shows the taxable amount. See how pensions are taxed.
    • Itemized deductions, credits, capital gains, and the 3.8% net investment income tax.
    • State rules. We apply your state rate to your pension, withdrawals and other income only, because Oregon, Washington and most states don't tax Social Security. Oregon also offers a subtraction for federal pensions earned before October 1991 and a few credits for retirees.
    • Medicare surcharges. Higher incomes pay more for Parts B and D. See IRMAA.

    To set withholding on a pension, use IRS Form W-4P with your retirement system (federal retirees use OPM's Services Online). For Social Security, use Form W-4V. Our guides explain how Social Security is taxed and how to build a retirement budget.

    See a worked example

    A married couple, both 65 or older, has a $42,000 pension, $12,000 a year from savings and $30,000 in Social Security. They pay $200 a month for health coverage and live in a state with no income tax.

    • Income before taxes: $7,000 a month.
    • Federal tax: about $355 a month, or about 5.1% of their income, thanks to the larger standard deduction at 65 and the senior deduction.
    • Take-home: about $6,445 a month after tax and health premiums.

    Common questions

    Is my pension taxed?

    Yes, by the federal government, as ordinary income. Part of each payment can be tax free if you paid into the pension with after-tax money. Your 1099-R shows the taxable amount.

    How much of my Social Security is taxed?

    Between 0% and 85%, depending on your other income. For many retirees with a modest pension, only part of it is taxed.

    Who gets the $6,000 senior deduction?

    People 65 and older, for tax years 2025 through 2028. It phases out at higher incomes. It's on top of the regular standard deduction.

    How do I have tax taken out of my pension?

    Give your retirement system IRS Form W-4P. For Social Security, use Form W-4V. Withholding avoids a big bill in April.

    An estimate, not tax advice. We aren't tax professionals. Ask a tax preparer or the IRS about your own return.
