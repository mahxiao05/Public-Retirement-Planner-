Our standards

What changed

Last updated October 24, 2026

Every update to the site, newest first: new guides and tools, the yearly figures we refresh, and any mistake we've fixed. Read how we check our numbers to see where the figures come from.

October 24, 2026

  • The Retirement Date Planner and Can I retire? now cover CalPERS (2% at 62, 2% at 55 and 2% at 60, with CalPERS's quarter-year benefit factors), CalSTRS (2% at 60 and 2% at 62, with the career factor) and Idaho PERSI (general and police or fire members, with the Rule of 90 or 80).
  • New: a Social Security calculator that shows your monthly amount at every age from 62 to 70.
  • New: Questions and answers, every guide's common questions on one page you can search.
  • Move your plan to another phone or computer: My plan and the planner can now copy a link or email it to you. No account needed, and your answers still never reach us.
  • A clearer menu: each menu opens into labeled columns, and on phones each section folds open with a tap.
  • The Retirement Date Planner now covers Washington DRS (PERS, TRS and SERS, Plans 1, 2 and 3), with early retirement cuts, survivor options and a Plan 3 or DCP account.
  • Calculator pages now include a worked example and common questions.
  • Shorter pages on phones: common questions and "On this page" open with a tap, and the glossary has a search box.
  • Guides and calculators now show who reviews them. Read about our editor on the About page.
  • Fixed: "How long will my savings last?" now says "1 month" instead of "1 months".

October 18, 2026

  • New: Can I retire?, a quick answer with Social Security at 62, full retirement age or 70, for Oregon PERS, Washington DRS, FERS or any pension.
  • New calculators: FERS sick leave, FERS military buyback, single life or survivor option, take-home pay after taxes and how long savings last.
  • New systems: Washington DRS, CalPERS, CalSTRS and Idaho PERSI.
  • 36 new guides on Oregon PERS, FERS and the TSP, Social Security, Medicare, survivor options, lump sums and taxes, plus a glossary of 100 retirement words and Retirement numbers for 2027.
  • Every older guide now has a worked example and common questions. Corrected: Oregon PERS final average salary is the higher of your three best years or your last 36 months; the FERS MRA+10 cut is waived with 20 years when the annuity starts at 60 or later; a FERS survivor annuity smaller than the full one needs your spouse's consent.
  • The countdown checklist now dates each step and adds them to your calendar. My plan shows everything you've saved on your device.
  • Added this page and How we check our numbers.
  • Added a "Keep reading" list to the end of every guide and grouped the guides page by topic.
  • Added a site icon and a page for addresses that don't exist.

October 17, 2026

  • New look built for phones, with a tab bar along the bottom of the screen.
  • New complete guides: Where to begin, Oregon PERS explained, Social Security explained and FERS explained.
  • Added the Retirement countdown checklist and the guide to health insurance before 65.
  • Added tap-to-open "i" buttons that explain each number, with links to the official source.
  • Updated Social Security figures for 2027: bend points of $1,340 and $8,077 and an earnings limit of $25,560.
  • Launched the Retirement Date Planner (Oregon PERS or FERS, Social Security, spouse options and Money Match) and the Oregon PERS Pension Estimator.
