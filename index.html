About this site

Retirement rules are long and hard to read. This site turns the official formulas into a few simple questions and a plain answer. You can open "How we worked this out" at the end of any tool to see each step of the math and the source for every number.

We check the figures against Oregon PERS (oregon.gov/pers) and the Social Security Administration (ssa.gov), and update them when those agencies publish new numbers each year. Each tool shows the date its rules were last checked.

This site gives estimates, not advice. We aren't part of Oregon PERS, Social Security or any government agency. Before you choose a retirement date, ask PERS and Social Security for official estimates.
