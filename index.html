Your path to retirement

Four steps most people take in the last few years before they retire.

  1. Get your records

    Your years of service, your pay and your Social Security statement.

  2. Compare dates

    See what you'd get at your earliest date, your full-benefit date and 62 or 65.

  3. Plan health care

    Line up coverage from your retirement date until Medicare at 65.

  4. Apply on time

    PERS, OPM, Social Security and Medicare each have their own deadline.

How we keep our numbers right

  • Official sources onlyEvery rule comes from Oregon PERS, OPM, the TSP or Social Security, with a link so you can check it.
  • Updated when the rules changeSocial Security figures each fall, PERS factors each January, TSP limits each year.
  • IndependentWe're not a government agency, and we don't sell investments or insurance.
  • PrivateYour answers stay on your own device. Read how we work.
