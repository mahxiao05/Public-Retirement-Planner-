Your path to retirement

Four steps most people take in the last few years before they retire.

  1. Get your records

    Your years of service, your pay and your Social Security statement.

  2. Compare dates

    See what you'd get at your earliest date, your full-benefit date and 62 or 65.

  3. Plan health care

    Line up coverage from your retirement date until Medicare at 65.

  4. Apply on time

    PERS, OPM, Social Security and Medicare each have their own deadline.

Common questions

Is this site run by the government?

No. Public Retirement Planner is an independent site owned by X Ventures LLC. We build our tools from the rules that Oregon PERS, Washington DRS, OPM, Social Security and other agencies publish, and link to them so you can check.

Which retirement systems do the calculators cover?

The Retirement Date Planner and Can I retire? cover Oregon PERS, Washington DRS (PERS, TRS and SERS Plans 1, 2 and 3), federal FERS, CalPERS (the three most common formulas), CalSTRS and Idaho PERSI. Every tool includes Social Security.

Do you save or see my answers?

No. Your answers stay in your own browser on your own device. We never receive them, and you can remove them at any time from My plan.

How accurate are the estimates?

They follow the official formulas, but they rely on the numbers you enter and leave out some special cases. Use them to compare choices, then ask your retirement system and Social Security for official estimates before you pick a date.

Does it cost anything?

No. The site is free and paid for by ads. Ads never change the math.

These are estimates, not advice. We aren't part of any government agency. Before you choose a retirement date, ask your retirement system and Social Security for official estimates.
